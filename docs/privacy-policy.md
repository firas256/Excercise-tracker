# Privacy Policy

**Rep Coach** (the iPhone app and its Apple Watch app) · Effective date: _[set on publication]_

Short version: **Rep Coach doesn't collect any data.** Everything it records stays on your iPhone,
your Apple Watch and, if you allow it, in Apple Health. There is no account, no advertising, no
analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything to the developer or anyone else.

## What the app works with

| Information | Where it comes from | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Motion sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope) | Your Apple Watch, during calibration and workouts | Processed on the watch. If "Save sensor data" is on (watch Settings), each confirmed set's motion is kept so you can replay it. |
| Workouts: exercises, sets, reps, weights you enter, form feedback | The app | On your iPhone and Apple Watch |
| Calibration and estimated body measurements (arm length, forearm, height estimate, reference angles) | Calculated on the watch during calibration | On your iPhone and Apple Watch |
| Heart rate and active energy | Read from Apple Health during a workout, with your permission | Shown during the workout; the average is kept with the workout |
| Workouts saved to Apple Health | Written by the app, with your permission | Apple Health |

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange this information directly with each other using Apple's
WatchConnectivity. It isn't sent to any server run by the developer or a third party. The app
contains no networking code of its own.

## Apple Health

Rep Coach asks for permission to save strength-training workouts to Apple Health and to read heart
rate and active energy during workouts. Health data is used only to show you your workouts. It is
never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never
stored in iCloud by the app. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings ›
Privacy & Security › Health › Rep Coach.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Settings › Export my recordings creates a .zip of your motion recordings that you can
  share however you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.
- **Delete:** Settings › Delete all workouts / Delete recordings. Deleting the app removes everything
  it stored on your devices. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them
  in the Health app.
- **Backups:** like any app, the app's data is part of your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup or a
  computer) if you use them. These are controlled by you and Apple, not the developer.

## Children

Rep Coach collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
