# Help & Support

## What you need
- iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later, paired.
- Allow Apple Health access when asked: it lets the watch keep tapping you and updating your iPhone
  while your wrist is down (watchOS only allows that during a workout session).

## Getting good results
- **Calibrate** at the start of a workout. Keep the iPhone app open so you can follow the steps on
  its screen. Keep your arm straight when raising it forward: that step measures your arm length.
- **Wear the watch snugly**, on the wrist you told the Watch app about.
- **Lock the exercise** (watch: swipe left; iPhone: tap the guess) for machines where your hands
  don't move much, like the leg press.
- **Correct wrong guesses** in the set summary. The app learns your form from every correction.
- Reps covering less than half of the full range aren't counted. The watch gives a different tap
  and shows "Too short – not counted".

## Common questions
**The watch stops tapping when my wrist is down.** Health access for Workouts is probably off.
Turn it on in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Rep Coach. The watch and the iPhone
show an orange note with the reason when this happens.

**The exercise guess is wrong.** Tap the right one (watch or iPhone), or lock it before the set.
Some exercises look almost the same from the wrist (for example a lateral raise and a front raise);
your corrections teach the app the difference.

**Can I export or delete my data?** Yes: iPhone Settings › Export my recordings / Delete all workouts.
See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
